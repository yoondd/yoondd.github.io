In 2025, I decided to challenge myself to earn the Korean Information Security Engineer (정보보안기사) certification — one of the most respected credentials for cybersecurity professionals in Korea.  
This certification focuses on a wide range of topics such as network security, cryptography, secure coding, risk management, and system security.

As a software quality and security engineer, I wanted to strengthen my foundation not only in static analysis and secure coding justification but also in the broader security governance and infrastructure aspects.
